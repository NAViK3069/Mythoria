# Mythoria — notes for Claude

- Web-based Pixel 2.5D turn-based fantasy RPG (exploration like AFK Journey, combat like Honkai: Star Rail), playable on PC / mobile / iPad browsers. Online features later: story, PvP, guild, boss raid, payments.
- Owner writes in Thai; project docs are in Thai.
- Read `docs/03-roadmap.md` for the current phase and `docs/04-folder-structure.md` for where code goes.
- Game design is decided by the owner — don't invent lore, characters or mechanics; ask, or leave placeholders in `docs/game-design/`.
- Rules: `packages/combat` is pure deterministic TypeScript (seeded RNG, no Phaser/DOM). Game numbers live in `packages/game-data`, not in code. Client talks to a `GameService` interface (Local now, Online later).
- Owner's earlier games are the references: battle rules come from https://github.com/NAViK3069/LineRanger_TurnBased ; the character model (head/body/2 legs on a 32px frame, floating hands, no arms, animate by moving parts by whole pixels) from https://github.com/NAViK3069/ProjectZ . Characters face left/right only — no back view. See `docs/game-design/characters.md`.
- The master character only walks the map; heroes (party) fight in battle.
- Art: map art comes from PixelLab (owner has a Pro plan; MCP tools `mcp__pixellab__*`). Picked sources live in `assets/world/meadow/`; `python tools/build_meadow_assets.py [download_dir]` packs them into `apps/client/public/assets/world/`. Animated effects are our own code in `apps/client/src/fx/effects/` (butterflies, fires), drawn on the pixelfx engine (MIT) at map scale (1 unit = 1 px, no half-scale); `FxSprite` plays them, `world/Critters.ts` flies the butterflies. Still props (houses, tents, camp gear) come from PixelLab, named in `NAMED` in the build script; small props are generated on a 32 px canvas so they match the 32 px hero. Hitboxes are polygons per picture from `python tools/hitboxes.py` (auto from the picture + hand-drawn `MANUAL`; `--preview out.png` to check) → `world/hitboxes.json`; re-run it after adding art. `?hitboxes` in the URL draws them in game. Depth sorts on the footprint's front edge at the player's x. Each rule's `height` is the object's real height: footprint = picture minus that band; shadows are cast from each pixel's height above the footprint (`SUN_X/SUN_Y` in `MeadowView.ts`). Lily pads drift only, never rotate. Warp pads (one where each path leaves the map - gates to the next maps, locked until those exist; meanwhile they warp to each other): `world/Warps.ts` (flat rune pads + the jump) and `fx/effects/warp.ts` (our own design - pad, warp-out "ascension", warp-in "descent" - built from `MasterCharacter.snapshot()`), drawn on our own engine `fx/surface.ts` (no pixelfx); destinations panel in `ui/warp.ts`. PixelLab art that comes with a painted grass patch is listed in `GRASSY` (cut below its hitbox front edge) and toned with `TONE` in the build script, so it sits on our own ground in the map's colours. Keep `apps/client/public/THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.txt` up to date.
- UI is HTML/CSS over the canvas in `apps/client/src/ui/` (HUD, Modal panels, `store.ts` local sample data → GameService later). Icons/frame/key art (`assets/ui/art/`, placeholder scenes) from PixelLab, copied by `python tools/build_ui_assets.py <download_dir>`. Minimap is drawn once in `world/minimap.ts`; rewards show through `ui/reward.ts`. HUD layout (ADR 0004, the owner's): the bottom dock is two wings (bag, team | heroes, guild) either side of the big round PLAY-MODE emblem in the middle (`.play` + a small gold tag 'ต่อสู้' on its bottom rim, `.pl-ribbon` - not `.ribbon`, a class panels use; the owner found 'โหมดเล่น' + a sub-line odd); NO chat button - the chat is a feed in the bottom-left corner on PC (`.chat-feed`, as wide as fits beside the dock; the last 5 lines, 3 on a phone; tap opens the chat panel), on an upright phone top left just under the minimap (Hud.ts `placeChat`, follows the map folding); friends is a small button top right. Stepping ONTO a warp pad opens the warp panel by itself (no E; once per step on - `WorldScene.padRaw`; arriving by warp doesn't count).
